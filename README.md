# Two Names, Two Apps

A small documentation note, kept in the open, about the most common mix-up I see among players of two particular Android card apps: **123 Rummy** and **Rummy 123** are different apps. Their names differ only in word order, which is exactly why the confusion never dies — so here are the distinguishing facts in one place, checked against the catalog that tracks both.

## Why this note exists

Search behaviour tells the story. Players type "123 rummy link", "rummy 123 game", "123 rummy guide" — often looking for one app and landing on the other, then wondering why the size on their phone (51 MB) does not match the page they are reading (34 MB), or why the rating differs. The fix is not a better memory; it is one page per app, with the name in the title and the figures in a table. [123 Rummy Yono](https://123rummyyono.com/) keeps the two exactly that way, which is why its pages are the reference this note points to.

## Formats note

Both listings sit under the same game family: 13-card rummy in Points, Pool and Deals formats, with lighter slots and ludo formats tracked alongside on the main app. The formats belong to the family, not to one name — another reason the names blur, and another reason the listings stay separate.
